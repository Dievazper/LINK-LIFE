# Implementation Plan - Smart Gadget Launch

## Goal Description
Launch a new smart gadget. It is an intramuscular pulse sensor designed for **general public use** (simplifying health monitoring). It monitors heart rate, detects cardiac arrest, and automatically alerts emergency services via a subscription model. The installation is simple and requires no medical expertise.

## User Review Required
- **Visual Style**: Clean, modern, medical-tech aesthetic (accessible to all ages), not Cyberpunk.
- **Target Audience**: General population, specifically those concerned about heart health or with elderly relatives. *Investor focus for the ad.*

## Proposed Strategy

### 1. Product Definition
Refine the core concept to have broad appeal.
- **Product Name Options**: PulseNode, HeartGuard, LifeLink, CardioSense, VitalKeep.
- **Gadget**: Sub-dermal/Intramuscular Heart Rate Monitor.
- **Tech Stack**: IoT connectivity (LoRa/5G), Bio-impedance analysis, simple mobile app pairing.
- **Business Model**: Low hardware cost + Monthly "SafeHeart" Subscription.
