# Product Definition: LifeLink

**Product Name:** LifeLink  
**Slogan:** *Your Pulse, Connected. Your Life, Protected.*  
**Target Audience:** General public, specifically individuals with cardiovascular concerns, elderly users, and users with high-stress lifestyles.  
**Price Range:** €49.99 (Hardware) + €9.99/month (Service Subscription).

---

## 1. Main Problem Solved
Cardiovascular incidents often happen without warning. "Time is muscle"—every second counts during a cardiac arrest. Traditional wearables (watches) can be forgotten, run out of battery, or fail to detect medical-grade anomalies accurately. **LifeLink** solves this by being invisible, always-on, and directly connected to emergency services.

## 2. Technical Features (Realistic & IT-Related)
*   **Hardware:**
    *   **Bio-Impedance Sensor:** Sub-dermal biocompatible intramuscular sensor (size of a grain of rice).
    *   **Power Source:** Piezoelectric charging (harvests energy from muscle movement/blood flow) + ultra-long-life solid-state backup battery (10-year lifespan).
    *   **Connectivity:** Dual-mode LoRaWAN (Low Power Wide Area Network) for constant low-energy reporting and 5G/LTE-M emergency beacon for instant high-priority alerts.
    *   **Processor:** ARM Cortex-M85 (Ultra-low power) with dedicated AI edge processing unit for arrhythmia detection.

*   **Software/Service:**
    *   **Edge AI Algorithm:** Filters noise and detects Ventricular Fibrillation (VF) and Asystole with 99.9% accuracy locally on the device.
    *   **LifeLink API:** Open API for integration with personal health dashboards (Apple Health, Google Fit) and medical provider systems.
    *   **Cloud Platform:** AES-256 encrypted health data storage compliant with GDPR/HIPAA.

## 3. Innovation & Competitive Advantage
*   **"Install & Forget":** Unlike smartwatches that need daily charging, LifeLink is a one-time simple installation.
*   **Direct-to-Dispatch:** Competitors alert a contact who *then* calls 911/112. LifeLink integrates directly with emergency dispatch systems (E911/E112) sending geolocation and vitals instantly.
*   **Affordability:** The "Loss Leader" hardware model (€49.99) makes advanced medical monitoring accessible to everyone, not just the wealthy.

## 4. Installation Process (Simplified)
1.  **Prep:** Clean the area (upper arm).
2.  **Inject:** Use the automated, sterile applicator (painless spring-loaded system).
3.  **Link:** Tap smartphone to the sensor via NFC to activate and pair with the LifeLink app.
