Section 1

5-Minute Pitch Script Excerpt

[Start Timestamp 0:00]

"Every 40 seconds, someone in the United States has a heart attack. And the most terrifying part isn't the attack itself—it's the silence that follows. The delay between the onset of Ventricular Fibrillation and the arrival of an ambulance is where lives are lost. It's called the 'fatal gap.'


We are Ricard and Diego, and we created LinkLife to eliminate that gap permanently. LinkLife is the end of too late.


Today, a smartwatch might tell your phone you're having an irregular heartbeat. But if you're unconscious, your phone sits quietly in your pocket while time runs out. Our wearable is different. The LinkLife sensor continuously monitors high-fidelity ECG. When it detects a critical cardiac event, it doesn't just buzz a notification—it autonomously triggers a cellular connection, transmits your exact GPS location, and relays a live telemetry feed directly to the nearest dispatch center within 400 milliseconds."

Section 2

Contest 3-Point Winning Argument

01

Zero Dependency Protocol

Unlike Apple Watch or Fitbit, LinkLife doesn't rely on the user being conscious to call 911. The hardware makes the call autonomously via built-in eSIM.

02

Pre-hospital Triage

EMTs don't arrive blind. They receive the patient's live ECG curve and historical vitals before they get out of the ambulance, reducing time-to-shock.

03

Unmatched B2C Economics

By offering the hardware as a €49.99 loss-leader, we unlock a massive €9.99/mo subscription model, yielding 85% gross margins at scale.
