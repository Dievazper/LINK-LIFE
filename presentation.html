Section 1

5-Minute Pitch Script Excerpt

"Every 40 seconds, someone in the United States has a heart attack. The delay between the onset of Ventricular Fibrillation and the arrival of an ambulance is where lives are lost. It's called the 'fatal gap.'


Our intramuscular chip is different. The LinkLife sensor continuously monitors high-fidelity ECG directly from inside the muscle. When it detects a critical cardiac event, it relays a live telemetry feed directly to the nearest dispatch center within 400 milliseconds via the connected smartphone application."

Procediment d'Implantació (How it's applied)

Un procediment mínimament invasiu de 5 minuts amb anestèsia local.

1. Preparació: S'aplica anestèsia local a la zona del deltoide.
2. Injecció: L'aplicador especialitzat insereix la càpsula a la capa muscular.
3. Retirada: L'aplicador es retira deixant el sensor actiu de per vida.

Intramuscular Chip (3D Architecture)

Càpsula de titani i polímer biocompatible. Fes clic i arrossega per rotar.

Antenna (Top)

Core (Middle)

Bateria micro-cinètica de llarga durada i processador ECG neural.

Optic Sensor (Bottom)

Lector intra-muscular directe d'impulsos cardiovasculars i oxigen.
