The Sub-Dermal Architecture

A titanium and biocompatible polymer capsule. Drag to rotate and inspect the internal components.

Antenna (Top)

NFC and Bluetooth LE to communicate securely with your smartphone.

Core (Middle)

Long-lasting micro-kinetic battery and neural ECG processor.

Optic Sensor (Bottom)

Direct intra-muscular reader for cardiovascular impulses and oxygen levels.

How It's Applied

A minimally invasive 5-minute procedure under local anesthesia.

1. Prep: Local anesthesia applied to the deltoid.
2. Injection: Specialized applicator inserts the capsule.
3. Withdrawal: Applicator removed, leaving sensor active for life.
