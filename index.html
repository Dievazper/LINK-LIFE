For our friend, for everyone.

LinkLife wasn't born in a corporate boardroom. It was born from a tragedy. A close friend of ours suffered a sudden heart attack while he was alone. Because no one was notified in time, the medical response arrived too late. This "fatal gap" of silence cost him his life.

Ricard and Diego swore that this would never happen to anyone else. We dedicated our lives to engineering a solution that removes human dependency from emergency alerts. We built an automated lifeline so that no one ever dies waiting for help again.

The Sub-Dermal Architecture

A titanium and biocompatible polymer capsule. Drag to rotate and inspect the internal components.

Antenna (Top)

NFC and Bluetooth LE to communicate securely with your smartphone.

Core (Middle)

Long-lasting micro-kinetic battery and neural ECG processor.

Optic Sensor (Bottom)

Direct intra-muscular reader for cardiovascular impulses and oxygen levels.

How It's Applied

A minimally invasive 5-minute procedure under local anesthesia.

1. Prep: Local anesthesia applied to the deltoid.
2. Injection: Specialized applicator inserts the capsule.
3. Withdrawal: Applicator removed, leaving sensor active for life.
