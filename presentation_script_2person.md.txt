# LifeLink: Oral Presentation Script (2 Presenters)

**Time Estimate:** 5-6 Minutes
**Characters:**
*   **ALEX (The Visionary):** Focuses on the emotional impact, the problem, and the "why."
*   **SAM (The Tech Specialist):** Focuses on the "how," the specs, the installation, and the business model.

---

**(0:00 - 0:45) INTRODUCTION & THE HOOK**

**ALEX:** (Standing center, grave tone) 
Imagine this. You are alone at home. Maybe you’re watching TV, or working late on your computer. Suddenly, your chest tightens. You feel dizzy. 
(Pause)
You try to reach for your phone, but it’s just out of reach. You try to call out, but you can’t breath. 
In that moment, your smartwatch—if you remembered to charge it, if you are wearing it—might beep. But who hears it?

**SAM:** (Steps forward)
Every year, thousands of people die from cardiac arrests that happen when they are alone. They call it the "Silent Killer." 
Current technology fails us because it relies on *us*. It relies on us wearing a device, charging a battery, and pressing a button.

**ALEX:**
But what if the technology didn't need *you* to do anything? What if it was just... part of you?

**(0:45 - 1:30) INTRODUCING LIFELINK**

**ALEX:**
We are proud to introduce **LifeLink**. 
(Both point to the screen/visual aid showing the LifeLink logo)
LifeLink isn’t a wearable. It’s an *installable*.

**SAM:**
It is a sub-dermal, intramuscular pulse sensor. It’s about the size of a grain of rice. Once installed, it becomes a permanent, invisible guardian for your heart.

**ALEX:**
No wristbands. No chest straps. No chargers. 
You live your life, and LifeLink watches over you, 24/7/365.

**(1:30 - 2:30) HOW IT WORKS (THE DEMO)**

**SAM:**
Let’s talk about how it works. 
(Gestures to the "Installation" slide)
The installation takes less than 60 seconds. It’s painless—like a quick pinch. We use a sterile, pre-loaded applicator. You place it on the upper arm, press, and it’s done.

**ALEX:**
And once it’s there?

**SAM:**
It wakes up. 
Using **Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting**, it powers itself from the natural movement of your muscles and blood flow. It never needs a battery change for at least 10 years.

**ALEX:**
(Smiling)
So I can forget it’s there?

**SAM:**
Exactly. That’s the point. 
But LifeLink doesn’t forget you. It uses an embedded **ARM Cortex-M85** processor with Edge AI. It learns *your* specific heart rhythm. 
If it detects a lethal arrhythmia—like Ventricular Fibrillation—it doesn’t just beep. It acts.

**(2:30 - 3:30) CONNECTIVITY & ALERTS**

**ALEX:**
Most devices connect to your phone via Bluetooth. But what if your phone is in the other room? What if it’s dead?

**SAM:**
LifeLink has a dual-connectivity stack. 
1.  **LoRaWAN:** For constant, low-power health stats sent to your doctor or our cloud dashboard.
2.  **Emergency 5G Beacon:** In a crisis, effectively bypassing your phone, it connects directly to the nearest cell tower to broadcast a "Code Red" distress signal.

**ALEX:**
This signal goes directly to emergency dispatchers—911 or 112. 
It sends your GPS coordinates, your identity, and your live ECG data. The ambulance is dispatched before you even hit the floor.

**(3:30 - 4:30) BUSINESS MODEL & MARKET**

**ALEX:**
Now, you might be thinking: "This sounds expensive. High-tech medical implants are for millionaires."

**SAM:**
Not LifeLink. 
Our mission is universal access. 
We have adopted a "Loss Leader" strategy for the hardware.
The device itself costs just **€49.99**. That’s cheaper than a video game.

**ALEX:**
We make it accessible so anyone can afford the protection. 
The real value comes from our service. 
**The "SafeHeart" Subscription.**
For **€9.99 a month**—less than a Netflix subscription—you get the 24/7 active monitoring, the direct line to emergency services, and the peace of mind.

**SAM:**
We target three main groups:
1.  **High-risk individuals:** Heart patients.
2.  **The Elderly:** Who often forget to wear devices.
3.  **High-stress professionals:** Like us in IT, who work long hours and neglect our health.

**(4:30 - 5:15) COMPETITION & CONCLUSION**

**ALEX:**
So, why is LifeLink the "Best Overall Gadget"?
Apple Watch measures your heart beats... sometimes.
Fitbit tracks your steps.
LifeLink saves your life.

**SAM:**
It is invisible. It is immortal (battery-wise). And it is intelligent.

**ALEX:**
We aren’t just selling a gadget. We are selling the future of personal health. 
A future where technology doesn’t ask for your attention. It just protects your existence.

**BOTH:**
Connect with LifeLink. Your Pulse, Connected.

**(End of Presentation)**
